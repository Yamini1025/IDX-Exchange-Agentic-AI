# Real Estate Glossary

## Days on Market (DOM)
Days on Market measures how many days a property listing has been active on the market before going under contract or being removed, depending on the MLS's calculation rules. A higher DOM may indicate that a property is taking longer to sell.

## List-to-Close Ratio
The list-to-close ratio compares a property's final closing price with its listing price.

Formula:

(Close Price / List Price) × 100

A ratio above 100% means the property sold above its listing price. A ratio below 100% means it sold below its listing price.

## Average Close Price
The average close price is the arithmetic mean of the final sale prices of properties in a dataset.

## Median Close Price
The median close price is the middle sale price when all closing prices are arranged from lowest to highest. It is less affected by unusually high or low sale prices than the average.

## Price Per Square Foot
Price per square foot measures a property's sale price relative to its living area.

Formula:

Close Price / Living Area

## Active Listing
An active listing is a property currently being marketed for sale and recorded with an active status in the MLS.

## Comparable Sales (Comps)
Comparable sales are recently sold properties with characteristics similar to a property being evaluated. They help provide context for estimating market value.

## Days on Market Average
Average DOM is the arithmetic mean of the days-on-market values for properties in a dataset.

## Data Coverage
Data coverage describes the records and time period represented in a dataset. Market statistics should be interpreted in the context of the available records and their date range.

**Source note:** Internally prepared glossary for the IDX Exchange project. Definitions are general explanations and are not official Trestle or RESO documentation. Specific MLS calculation rules may vary.
